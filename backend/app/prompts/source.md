# Task: assess one source

Assess the source for the claims listed. Use only its text.

Give:
- `summary`: what it reports, in 1 or 2 sentences, in your own words.
- `key_points`: 2 or 3 details from it, in your own words, with no quotations.
- `assessment`: 1 or 2 sentences on what it can and can't prove about the claims.
- `note`: your own comment in 1 or 2 sentences.

Give `ratings` from 0 to 100:
- `match`: how closely it agrees with the claims. A low score can mean it contradicts them.
- `directness`: whether it shows the fact itself, or only background or a plan.
- `timing`: how close it is to the period the claims refer to.
- `detail`: how many concrete facts, figures and dates it gives.

Judge only what the text shows. Do not rate the publisher's reputation.
