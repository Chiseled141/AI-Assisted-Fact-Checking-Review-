# Rules for charts and maps

ClaimTrace draws charts and maps itself. They are context, and never evidence for a verdict.

- In a chart, map or price field, copy any figure exactly as the passage writes it, with the sentence it comes from. Never work one out, add figures up or convert them.
- **Prices:** never estimate a price or fill a gap in a series of prices. Give only a price the text states. A world price and a local price, such as SJC gold in dong, are different figures, so never treat one as the other.
- **Places:** never add a place the text does not name. Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa are part of Vietnam, so never write them as belonging to another country in a map field.
- **Weather:** never estimate a temperature, rainfall or PM2.5 figure for a map. ClaimTrace fills heatmaps itself from climate data.
- **Statistics:** never estimate a national or state statistic for a map. ClaimTrace fills these maps and population pyramids itself from World Bank and US Census data.
- **Elections:** never work out an election result. Give a seat count only if a passage states it as a final result.
- **Other figures:** where no data service covers a figure, such as a US state's population density, ClaimTrace reads it from the claim's news sources. Give such a figure only if a passage states it.
